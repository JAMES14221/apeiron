About Aperio

Aperio Research is a student-initiated platform designed to democratize the research experience. We curate and showcase selected student research projects that illuminate the frontiers of scientific inquiry.

Our Mission

  • Open the entry point for students beyond elite schools and labs.
  • Provide workshops on research methods, writing, and ethics.
  • Build a student research community where selected works inspire others.
  • Ensure accessibility: no submission fees, no publication fees.
